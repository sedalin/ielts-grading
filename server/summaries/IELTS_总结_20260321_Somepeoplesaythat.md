## 原文
Nowadays, there is an ongoing debate on how we should protect our environment. While some people believe that the extinction of certain species is the most vital problem, others reckon that there are more serious issues such as climate change like air pollution.

On the one hand, some people worry about the extinction of some species primarily because every plant and animal has its own role in the ecosystem. When one type disappears, it can break the whole biological chain.Take dung beetles as an example, they are crucial because they help decompose the feces of livestocks. If dung beetles disappeared, the grassland would all be covered with feces. Then it would lead to more detrimental effects such as the decrease in biodiversity.

On the other hand, many people argue that there are bigger environmental issues that have more negative impacts. They state that climate change, air pollution and deforestation are more important as they affect the earth and our lives more directly. For instance, global warming makes icebergs melt, which destroys the home of polar bears. Furthermore, it also leads to rise of sea level, flooding major coastal cities in Japan and England. Last but not least, with technology evolving, the number of factories in the cities grows significantly. As a result, pollution in some large cities has already contaminated the air, making people more likely to contract respiratory diseases. Therefore, these people believe that we should focus on these sorts of issues first.

From my perspective, although the disruption of biological system and biodiversity cannot be experienced by normal people who live in the city, the gradual impact is still detrimental in the long term. By contrast, the influence of environmental issues such as pollution and climate change are more direct and they need immediate attention.
